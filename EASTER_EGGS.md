# The RORMS Book of Anomalies

This is the definitive list of all **50 unique Easter Eggs** hidden within the infinite Dean's Book. 

Each time you flip through a chunk of the book, you have a chance of encountering one of these anomalies. Because the system draws from a non-repeating "deck", you will never see the same anomaly twice within a single loop!

### The Original 11
1. **CLASSIFIED Document**: An SCP-style redacted file warning of a threat at large.
2. **Hacker Terminal**: A green CLI interface trying to decrypt the local node.
3. **Wild West Bounty**: A "Wanted Dead or Alive" poster offering ₿50,000,000.
4. **Missing Pet Poster**: Answers to the department code. "Bites. Do not make eye contact."
5. **Fortune Cookie Slip**: A tiny piece of paper with the cursed numbers: 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42.
6. **Blue Screen of Death**: A Windows Fatal Exception 0E crash.
7. **Tarot Card**: The XIII - The End card.
8. **Intergalactic Dating Profile**: "Looking for someone to help grade papers. 4 lightyears away."
9. **Ransom Note**: "We have him" cut out from assorted magazines.
10. **Retro Game Screen**: A Gameboy-style "RORMS: Press Start" screen.
11. **Horror Warning**: A blood-splattered wall telling you "They are already in the walls. LEAVE NOW."
