# RORMS User Acceptance Testing Questionnaires

## How to administer

Ask each participant to complete **Section A** and the section for their assigned role. The role called **University Administrative** in the testing plan is labeled **University Administrator** in RORMS. Present each statement after the participant has tried the relevant task. Allow **N/A** when a task or feature was unavailable or not tested; do not treat N/A as a neutral response.

**Response scale:** 1 = Strongly disagree; 2 = Disagree; 3 = Neither agree nor disagree; 4 = Agree; 5 = Strongly agree; N/A = Not applicable or not tested.

Record the participant's role, test date, device (desktop/mobile), browser, and any task that could not be completed. Use the same test data and task instructions for participants in the same role. All statements are positively worded, so higher scores indicate better acceptance. Analyze Section A and each role section separately; exclude N/A responses from averages and report their frequency.

## Optional written feedback (not part of the Likert score)

These prompts capture specific problems and improvement ideas that a numerical rating may not explain.

1. Which task was hardest to complete, and what happened?
2. Which information or feature was missing for your role?
3. Did any displayed schedule, room, or reservation information appear incorrect? Please describe it.
4. What one change would most improve your experience?
