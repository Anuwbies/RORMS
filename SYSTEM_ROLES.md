# RORMS - Room and Schedule Management System

## System Purpose
The **Room and Schedule Management System (RORMS)** is a centralized platform designed to streamline the complex process of academic scheduling. Its primary objective is to manage the mapping of instructors to specific courses, time slots, and physical rooms while preventing conflicts and optimizing campus resource utilization.

---

## User Roles & Responsibilities

### 1. System Administrator (Admin)
The Admin ensures the technical integrity and foundational data of the system.
*   **User Management:** Create, update, and deactivate user accounts (Registrar, Dean, Instructor).
*   **Master Data Management:** Define and manage the list of Buildings, Rooms (including capacity and equipment), and Departments.
*   **System Configuration:** Set global constraints, such as standard time blocks, semester start/end dates, and holiday blackout periods.
*   **Audit Logs:** Monitor system activity to ensure data security and accountability.
*   **Room Reservations:** An accepted Admin's valid room reservation is approved immediately. The three recommendation flags are completed automatically and the reservation records Admin as the approver.

### 2. Registrar (Room Plotter & Global Scheduler)
The Registrar is the central authority responsible for campus-wide resource allocation and finalizing the master schedule.
*   **Room Allocation (Plotting):** Review proposed schedules from all departments and plot them into actual physical rooms.
    In Department Schedules, clicking an Instructor or Program Head opens ScheduleModal with a room dropdown on each saved meeting. Plotting means the Registrar assigns rooms to faculty schedules supplied without Rooms & Labs room assignments; the Save button commits the selected rooms together. Saving a room assignment makes that schedule Plot immediately, including partially assigned classes; unassigned meeting cards stay grey. Clearing the last assigned room returns the schedule to Draft. The same meeting shown in several calendar slots shares one room choice. Room status, capacity, and overlapping classes in the selected term are checked again when plotting. Room booking days and hours apply only to reservations. Accepted Registrar/Admin memberships can plot during Drafting, Plotting, Revision, or Final. Registrar access to department workbooks is read-only in every phase: Save and the toolbox are hidden, Ctrl/Cmd+S is blocked, and sheet options expose only View comments. All phases remain selectable for viewing. Admin workbook editing and Dean/Program Head department editing retain their existing phase permissions. The deprecated Schedule Setup dropdown has been removed from the workbook toolbox.
    DepartmentScheduleWorkbook's workbook Save writes top-level `providedRoomKeys` on each schedule from its explicit room entries, including TBA and unresolved room names. Blank room cells are unprovided. PlotScheduleModal reads and preserves this source list: resolved provided rooms retain a read-only room label and checkbox in both Draft and Plot; provided TBA/unresolved rooms are read-only and must be changed in the department workbook. Checking stages Plot approval; unchecking an approved schedule stages Draft without clearing its room. Plot or Save Changes commits the staged status, and Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y supports both directions. When Faculty, Time, Day, or Room contains multiple entries, workbook Save creates separate saved meetings paired by position while keeping the joined workbook cells. Each day has its own checkbox and Draft/Plot status. A single room value starts both days in the same room; distinct plotted rooms are written as slash-separated values in that room cell. Legacy `registrarPlot.providedRoomKeys` remains readable until the next workbook save; older records without either list must be saved from the workbook before a new room can be assigned. Blank-source rooms newly selected by the Registrar retain the editable picker after Plot; a later department workbook Save classifies its current explicit room values again. Checking alone does not change saved status. Cards use saved status, so Draft remains grey even with an assigned room and Plot is green.
    For the compact department workbook, Plot commits room changes to both the `schedules` record and the workbook's F2F Room cells. Reopening the workbook shows the updated rooms. Department workbook saves keep unchanged Plot schedules approved; only edited schedules return to Draft, and new schedules start Draft. Room cells are authoritative: clearing one also clears that meeting's saved room/building IDs and Registrar assignment. Registrar access remains limited to room plotting; subject edits and other workbook cells retain their existing role permissions.
    Plot checkboxes always appear at the top right of compact workbook schedule cards, including cards with editable room pickers. Missing rooms show the search... placeholder; explicit workbook TBA remains read-only. Deleting all text from an editable room picker clears its selection, while focusing or typing a nonempty search preserves it. Every new or changed room assignment requires a manual checkbox check before Plot, including ordinary schedules. Editing a room revokes its row approval; checking approves the displayed day or parallel group. The save service rejects unchecked compact room assignments as well as unchecked parallel groups. A missing room keeps its checkbox unchecked and disabled. Registrar-assigned rooms can also be approved or returned to Draft with the checkbox while retaining the editable picker and room choice. A parallel card has one checkbox for its entire matching instructor/subject/day/time/format group. It stays unchecked and disabled until every section has a resolved room and the rooms form a connected chain. Assigning individual rooms or a recommended room chain only enables the checkbox; the user must check it manually, then click Plot to approve the group. Editing a room clears the group's staged approval while retaining its choices. Plot remains disabled while parallel choices await that manual check, and the save service rejects parallel requests without an explicit target status. Checking or unchecking stages every section together as one undo/redo action. Plot expands explicitly approved parallel saves to all member rows, applies the selected day's Draft/Plot status across that group, and rechecks every member's permissions, revision, rooms and conflicts before committing the group atomically.
*   **Conflict Resolution:** Identify and resolve cross-department double-bookings, overlapping instructor schedules, and room capacity issues.
    Plot approval checkboxes cannot be checked while their schedule has a live validation error. A row-level checkbox checks errors across its meetings; a parallel checkbox checks every member. Correcting the error restores checking. Already-approved schedules can still be unchecked to return to Draft without clearing rooms, even if an overlap is present.
*   **Publishing:** Finalize and approve the "Proposed" departmental schedules, pushing them live for instructors and students to view.
*   **Ad-hoc Adjustments:** Manage mid-semester room changes or emergency rescheduling due to maintenance.
*   **Reporting:** Generate reports on room utilization, peak-time usage, and overall scheduling efficiency.
*   **Member Management:** Use the Members tab to invite, edit, deactivate, re-invite, and cancel invitations through the same audited member-management callable as Admins. The callable requires an active, accepted Registrar membership and records Registrar as the actor role.

### 3. Dean (Department Head / Academic Planner)
The Dean acts as the primary academic planner for their specific department.
*   **Schedule Creation:** Create the initial draft schedule for their department, mapping subjects, instructors, sections, times, and days.
    Department creation uses the supplied Faculty Schedules and PHINMA Department Courses Excel templates together, with Rooms & Labs as an optional third sheet. The faculty timetable supplies instructor/day/time/subject/section; the course table supplies title, year level, credits, and subject type. The room table supplies room codes for uniquely matching faculty meetings, including separate rooms for adjacent time slots. Original templates remain editable in their existing layouts. Save regenerates schedule records from those cells: Plot when at least one meeting has a resolved Rooms & Labs assignment, otherwise Draft for later Registrar plotting. Lecture and Laboratory means Flexible/Open Lab, with delivery chosen by the instructor; the system does not invent a lecture/lab hour split. Meetings without a matching room remain TBA for later Registrar plotting. Existing department/semester editing permissions still apply.
*   **Manage Courses:** Accepted Deans and Program Heads can edit and save their department's complete courses workbook without choosing a semester. Save also writes populated rows to the `courses` collection; blank rows stay in the workbook. Admin memberships are permitted by the persistence service and Firestore rules. Course saves do not publish semester schedules.
*   **Manage Summary:** Accepted Deans and Program Heads can edit and save their department's Summary tab in any semester phase. Schedule editing remains limited to Drafting and Revision.
*   **Room Requests:** Specify preferred rooms or building requirements for their departmental courses.
    Parallel sections sharing an instructor, subject, meeting type, days and time must use separate, consecutively numbered rooms in the same building and floor. Their rooms may form a chain such as PTC 300–301–302–303; each room need not directly adjoin the first section's room.
*   **Instructor Load Management:** Ensure that instructors are not over-scheduled and that their teaching hours align with department policies before submitting.
*   **Submission:** Submit the drafted departmental schedule to the Registrar as a "Proposed" schedule for room allocation.

### 4. Program Head
*   **Department Assignment:** A department may have multiple Program Heads, each represented by their own membership. Assigning or removing one does not change the others.
*   **Schedule Management:** Accepted Program Heads can edit their department's schedule during Drafting and Revision, and can be assigned teaching schedules. They can edit their department's Courses and Summary tabs in any semester phase.
*   **Member Management:** Program Heads can view department members; member-management actions remain restricted to the existing authorized roles.

### 5. Instructor
The Instructor is the end-user who utilizes the assigned resources to deliver education.
*   **Department Workbook:** Instructors can view their department's Schedule, Courses, and Summary tabs in every semester phase but cannot edit or save them.
*   **Schedule Viewing:** Access a personalized dashboard showing their assigned courses, rooms, and time slots.
*   **Availability Submission:** Submit "Preferred Teaching Times" or personal constraints (e.g., research blocks) for consideration during the scheduling phase.
*   **Change Requests:** Formally request a room or time change if the assigned resource does not meet the course's pedagogical needs.
*   **Room Reporting:** Report issues with room equipment (e.g., broken projector) directly through the system.

### 6. University Administrator
* **Department Assignment:** Always assigned to General Service Department. Invitations, role edits, signup, and reactivation preserve this fixed department.
* **Access:** Dashboard, Buildings & Rooms, Manage Reservations, Reserve a Room, My Schedule, My Department, My Reservations, Analytics, Reports, and Logs. My Schedule displays reservations only for this role. My Department displays the General Service Department's members without academic department cards, filters, or workbook controls; member edit/remove controls are disabled.
* **Dashboard:** A dedicated live facilities/reservations dashboard shows pending requests, today's reservations, building/room readiness, seating capacity, seven-day approved/pending demand, and shortcuts to its four other tabs. Dates use Philippine time. It reads only buildings, rooms, and reservations.
* **Analytics and Reports:** Only Reservations and Buildings & Rooms sections are visible; saved selections for Members or Schedules fall back to Reservations.
* **Facilities:** Can create, edit, and delete buildings and rooms, and moderate room comments through `manageFacility`. Collapsible room pairing in Buildings & Rooms also writes the two room links through a Firestore transaction so it persists with the currently deployed callable. Pairing requires consecutive trailing room-code numbers, the same code prefix, building, and floor.
* **Reservations:** Gives final approval or decline after Dean and Registrar recommendations through `updateReservationStatus`, including extended-reservation validation and audit logging.
* **Reserve a Room:** Admins, Registrars, and University Administrators can submit reservations alongside Deans, Program Heads, and Instructors. They can also manage reservation availability. Admin and University Administrator requests are approved automatically with all three recommendation flags satisfied, and the submitting role is recorded as the approver. Registrar requests remain Pending with Dean and Registrar recommendation flags satisfied at submission; the Registrar is recorded as the source of those flags, and only University Administrator approval remains.
* **Navigation:** Only the listed tabs are available. Member management and schedule/calendar management remain with their existing authorized roles.

## High-Level Workflow
All five roles can view Academic Calendar, including semester dates, phases, summaries, search, and filters. Only Admin and Registrar have calendar management controls (Manage Active Year, New School Year, Edit, and Delete); Dean, Program Head, and Instructor see the calendar in read-only mode.

Signed-in users across all roles can leave shared room comments through Buildings & Rooms to provide feedback or report equipment issues. Each comment displays its author and creation date. Users can delete their own comments and Admins or Registrars can delete any comment, with confirmation. The `manageFacility` callable verifies active accounts and author/moderator permissions and saves each deletion with an audit log. Accepted Admins/Registrars also use it for building/room management, including bulk room actions and rooms removed with a building. Existing permissive Firestore rules still allow direct writes outside this UI flow; those are not audited by this callable.
Unread room-comment notices and amber comment highlights are personal to each user. Opening the comments panel preserves highlights; clicking a comment marks only that comment seen. Closing/hiding the panel or closing Room Information marks the loaded discussion seen. The room card badge and comment-icon dot remain while unread feedback exists. Another user's actions do not clear them; read positions are private and persist across devices.
Users' own comments do not trigger unread notices; unread comments from other authors remain eligible even when the newest comment is their own.

1.  **Preparation:** Admin sets up the semester dates and ensures the room database is up to date.
2.  **Input & Academic Planning:** Deans draft their department's schedule (subjects, instructors, times, etc.) and submit them as "Proposed" schedules. Instructors may submit availability.
3.  **Plotting:** The Registrar reviews the proposed schedules and "plots" them into specific rooms on a global matrix, resolving any conflicts.
    Saved schedules take priority over room reservations: creating a schedule or changing its room/day/time/term automatically cancels overlapping Pending or Approved reservations within the semester dates and notifies their owners with the cancellation reason. This applies to saved Draft and Plot schedules; unsaved workbook edits do not cancel reservations.
4.  **Finalization:** Registrar publishes the final approved schedules.
5.  **Operation:** Instructors view their final assignments; the system manages any emergency changes during the semester.
    Users can submit single-day Extended reservations for exhibits and similar events, providing the booking Purpose and selecting start and end times. No separate extended-time reason is required. These requests require Dean recommendation followed by Registrar recommendation and University Administrator approval, may exceed standard duration and slot limits, and still obey room availability, operating hours, capacity, minimum duration, lead time, and conflict rules. Pending requests hold the interval. University Administrator approval rechecks current room constraints and booking/class conflicts on the server; saved academic schedules retain their existing priority.
    Semester phases automatically become Ended after their inclusive end date, checked daily at midnight in Philippine time. Admins and Registrars still manage the earlier scheduling phases. Overdue semesters in historical years are included, and the active-year selection is preserved.

## Reservation recommendation and final approval

Requests other than Admin and University Administrator submissions start Pending. Registrar submissions enter Pending with the Dean and Registrar flags complete and await University Administrator approval. For other Pending requests, the department Dean recommends first, setting `deanRecommend` while the request remains Pending, or declines the open request before recommending it with a required reason. The Registrar can then recommend or decline; Registrar recommendation sets `registrarRecommend` while the request remains Pending, and a decline requires a reason while leaving the Dean recommendation in place. Each reviewer can manually set their open or role-declined request to Pending, Approve, or Decline. Reapproving a role-declined request restores Pending and clears its decline metadata. Changing an earlier decision clears dependent later recommendations. The University Administrator gives final approval or decline after both recommendations; a final decline also requires a reason. Approval sets `universityAdministratorRecommend` and changes the status to Approved. The University Administrator's manual edit permits changing an Approved request to Declined with a reason, and can reopen only a request declined by the University Administrator. Reapproval clears the decline metadata; declining clears `universityAdministratorRecommend` while retaining the Dean and Registrar recommendations. Extended approvals and reapprovals use the callable's atomic conflict checks. Decline reasons are saved on the reservation, included in requester notifications, and recorded in audit logs. Program Heads do not have Manage Reservations access. Admin can manually set Pending, Approve, or Decline for the reservation's current review step; the edit updates the affected and later booleans, reservation status, decline metadata, and audit log in one callable transaction. The callable checks active accounts, accepted memberships, department scope, prior recommendations, and current status transactionally. Older open requests missing the flags can complete the steps in this order. A legacy Pending request with Registrar recommendation but no Dean recommendation returns to Registrar review after the Dean decides. Recommended requests hold rooms until approved, declined, cancelled, or expired, and saved academic schedules retain priority.

The manual status edit for each reviewer also offers Pending. It removes that role's positive decision, clears any later recommendations, and restores the request to Pending without a decline reason. A reset from Declined removes its saved decline metadata; a University Administrator reset from Approved reopens the request for final review. Dean and Registrar resets apply to their existing open or role-declined requests. Dean edits stop once the Registrar decides; Registrar edits stop once the University Administrator decides. Each Dean or Registrar recommendation notifies the requester and the next reviewer (accepted Registrars or accepted University Administrators). Final University Administrator approval notifies the requester.
