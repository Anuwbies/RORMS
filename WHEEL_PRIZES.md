# RORMS Wheel Spin Prizes

The Wheel of Fortune grants random prizes based on the department size tier (Good, Neutral, Bad). Below is the complete list of all possible rewards you can win when spinning the wheel!

### 🟢 Good Tier
*Awarded to the smallest departments (least crowded).*
- **iPhone 17 Pro Max** (`#f59e0b`)
- **Earn 5 🪙** (`#10b981`)
- **Earn 3 🪙** (`#3b82f6`)

### 🟡 Neutral Tier
*Awarded to average-sized departments.*
- **50 XP** (`#8b5cf6`)
- **25 XP** (`#ec4899`)
- **Spin Again!** (Refunds 1 coin) (`#6366f1`)

### 🔴 Bad Tier
*Awarded to the largest, most crowded departments.*
- **Spawn Imposter** (Spawns a hostile imposter on screen) (`#ef4444`)
- **Spawn Crew** (Spawns a crewmate on screen) (`#f43f5e`)
- **Nothing 😢** (`#64748b`)
